# A production incident

Do not invent an employer, a production fraud program, or a regulator conversation.
If you have not lived this, say so, and use a project or a laboratory example you can defend.

## Prompt

Tell me about a time that fits: A production incident.

## STAR scaffold

- Situation: [YOUR REAL EXAMPLE]
- Task: what decision or outcome was yours
- Action: [YOUR ACTION]
- Result: [YOUR RESULT]

Keep the result specific. A lesson with no result is a weaker answer. A result you cannot explain is a weaker answer still.
