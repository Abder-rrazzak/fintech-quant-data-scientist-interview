# Interview strategy

Lead with the decision, then the evidence, then the limit of the evidence.

## Spoken shape

Use the 30-second answer when the interviewer is checking orientation. Use the 2-minute answer when they want the design. Go to the deep dive only after they ask. The follow-ups in the question bank are the questions you should be ready to hear next.

## What to say about this repository

"I built a synthetic laboratory to practice the decision loop: target, horizon, leakage, baseline, cost threshold, and monitoring. The fraud rate and the default rate are properties of the generator, not of a portfolio I worked on."

## What not to say

- Do not describe the generator as a production fraud system you owned.
- Do not quote a metric from `artifacts/` as if it were a live book.
- Do not fill a behavioral answer with a story you did not live. Use `[YOUR REAL EXAMPLE]`.

## If you do not know

Say what you would measure, what data you would need, and which decision the answer would change. A precise gap is stronger than a vague model.
